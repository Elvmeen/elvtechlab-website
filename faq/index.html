Working with us

Getting started

What is ELV Tech Lab?

ELV Tech Lab, also known as ELV Lab, is a digital technology, corporate branding and Print Tech studio. We help businesses build useful digital products and present their brands consistently online and offline.

What services do you offer?

Our services include website design and development, software and apps, brand identity, corporate branding, graphic design, printing and custom branded merchandise. The right combination depends on your goals.

Who do you work with?

We work with startups, small and growing businesses, teams, organisations and established companies. We can discuss a project whether you need one deliverable or support across several areas.

How do I start a project?

Email admin@elvlab.info, call or WhatsApp +234 812 541 2914, or book a short introductory call. Tell us what you want to achieve and we will discuss the next step.

How do I request a quote?

Share a short project brief, required deliverables, quantity for print jobs, preferred deadline and any budget range you have. We will review the details and clarify anything we need before preparing a proposal or quote.

Digital services

Websites and software

Can you design and develop a new website?

Yes. We create responsive websites based on your goals, content and audience. We can discuss the structure, features, visual direction, domain and hosting requirements before work starts.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We can review an existing website and discuss improvements to its design, content, usability, performance or functionality. The work depends on the current setup and your priorities.

Do you build software and mobile apps?

We take on digital product and application work based on the project requirements. Share the problem you need to solve, the intended users and the key features so we can assess the best approach.

Do you provide domain names and hosting?

Domain registration and hosting needs are discussed as part of each website project. We will clarify what is included, what is billed separately and who will own the relevant accounts.

Branding and Print Tech

Design and production

What is corporate branding?

Corporate branding is the way your organisation presents itself across visual identity, documents, marketing materials, digital channels and other customer touchpoints. We help create a consistent, professional look.

Can you print branded materials and merchandise?

Yes. We support print and branded merchandise requests. Tell us the item, quantity, dimensions, preferred materials, artwork status and delivery location so we can confirm suitable options.

Can you design my materials before printing?

Yes. We can discuss design and production together, or review print-ready artwork you already have. We will confirm the design approval process before production.

What is the minimum order quantity for printing?

Minimum quantities depend on the product, material and production method. Contact us with the item and quantity you have in mind so we can advise you accurately.

Can I request changes after a design is shared?

Yes. The number and type of revisions will depend on the agreed project scope. We will clarify the feedback and approval process before work begins.

Pricing and delivery

Timelines and payment

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity, deliverables, production quantity and deadline. Contact us with your requirements and we will discuss the appropriate quote. We do not use one fixed price for every project.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary. A small design task may take less time than a website, software build or print production run. We confirm a realistic timeline after reviewing the requirements and dependencies.

When does work begin?

We begin after the scope, deliverables, timeline, payment terms and required materials have been agreed. Any project-specific deposit or milestone arrangement will be communicated before work starts.

Do you deliver printed orders?

Delivery options depend on the product, order size and destination. Share your location when requesting a quote so we can confirm whether delivery is available and any associated cost.
